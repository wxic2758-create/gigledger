Privacy notice

Pre-launch draft. This notice must be reviewed before launch.

When you use “Sign in with Google”, GigLedger stores your Google account email and name, plus the ledger entries you save. Your Google password is never stored.

You can export your saved ledger from the dashboard, then request account deletion by deleting your workspace after export. Account deletion removes your Google email, name, income entries, fixed costs, and workspace settings.

Unsaved calculations remain on the current page only and are cleared when you refresh or leave the page.

This pre-launch draft still requires the legal entity, contact details, retention period, processors, and final deletion workflow before general launch.
