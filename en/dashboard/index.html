How this prototype works

  1. 01
    Start with what you must pay

    Add one recurring business cost. GigLedger turns it into a daily floor before any client pays you.

  2. 02
    Record what you earn

    Add income and the platform fee. Net income updates immediately.

  3. 03
    See what is actually left

    True profit subtracts monthly fixed costs from net income. Tax reserve stays separate.
