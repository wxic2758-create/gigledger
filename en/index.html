Questions, answered.

The short version: we would rather be clear than overpromise.

Does GigLedger file my taxes?+

No. GigLedger gives you an estimate and a reserve target. It does not file returns or provide tax advice.

Does it connect to Stripe or the App Store?+

Official Stripe and App Store Connect sync is planned for Pro. Upwork, PayPal, and Fiverr can be imported with CSV. No bank passwords and no scraping.

What counts as a fixed cost?+

Subscriptions, software, rent, insurance, and hosting that recur. One-time purchases belong in expenses; do not record a subscription again as an expense.

Can I use it outside the US?+

The ledger works worldwide. You choose your reserve rate. The 25%-30% reference comes from common US self-employment practice, not your local tax law.

What happens to my data?+

After Sign in with Google, we store your Google email and name plus saved ledger data in Cloudflare D1. You can export and delete your account from the dashboard.

When will it launch?+

The first version is in development. You can try the calculator now, and sign in with Google to save your ledger.
