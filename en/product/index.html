The decisions it supports

AISubscription review

See what you actually spend on AI.

Start with templates for common AI tools. Before a charge, record whether you used it often, sometimes, or not at all. Two unused months are flagged for review, and each subscription links to the product and management page. We don’t claim to track usage automatically.

18Cash runway

How many days could you last?

Add cash on hand and compare it with your Daily Floor to see how long you can last without new work. With three full months of income, GigLedger also shows a three-month average reading.
